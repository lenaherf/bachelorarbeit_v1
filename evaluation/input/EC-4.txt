EC-4 — The registration process has alternative confirmation and rejection outcomes.

Stakeholder A:
Role: Employee
Description:
I send a registration request to the Training Coordinator.
After sending the registration request, I receive either a registration confirmation or a registration rejection from the Training Coordinator.
These are alternative responses to the same request. I receive only one of them.
If I receive a registration confirmation, I record the confirmed registration. This ends my part of the process.
If I receive a registration rejection, I record the rejected registration. This ends my part of the process.

Stakeholder B:
Role: Training Coordinator
Description:
I receive a registration request from the Employee.
I then check whether the Employee meets the participation requirements.
If the Employee meets the participation requirements, I send a registration confirmation to the Employee.
After sending the registration confirmation, I archive the approved registration request. This ends my part of the process.
If the Employee does not meet the participation requirements, I send a registration rejection to the Employee.
After sending the registration rejection, I archive the rejected registration request. This ends my part of the process.
The two outcomes are mutually exclusive. For each registration request, I send either a registration confirmation or a registration rejection, never both.
