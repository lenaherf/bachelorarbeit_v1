EC-3 - The coordinator describes two checks without specifying their order.

Stakeholder A:
Role: Employee
Description:
I send a registration request to the Training Coordinator.
I then receive an acknowledgement from the Training Coordinator.
After receiving the acknowledgement, I wait for and receive a registration confirmation from the Training Coordinator.
I then record the registration confirmation. This ends my part of the process.

Stakeholder B:
Role: Training Coordinator
Description:
I receive a registration request from the Employee.
I then send an acknowledgement to the Employee.
After sending the acknowledgement, I check whether a place is available and check whether the Employee meets the participation requirements.
I send the registration confirmation to the Employee after both checks are complete. After sending the registration confirmation, I close the registration request.
This ends my part of the process.
