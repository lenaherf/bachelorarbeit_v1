EC-1 - Both stakeholder contributions describe the same registration process consistently.

Stakeholder A:
Role: Employee
Description:
I send a registration request to the Training Coordinator. I then receive an acknowledgement from the Training Coordinator.
After receiving the acknowledgement, I wait for and receive a registration confirmation from the Training Coordinator.
I then record the registration confirmation. This ends my part of the process.

Stakeholder B:
Role: Training Coordinator
Description:
I receive a registration request from the Employee. I then send an acknowledgement to the Employee.
After sending the acknowledgement, I check the available training openings. Once i find an opening, I sign up the Employee to the training program.
Once I signed up the Employee, I send a registration confirmation to the Employee. After sending the registration confirmation, I close the registration request.
This ends my part of the process.
